Scalable Synthesis of a Curved Nanographene Hoop

The synthesis of cycloparaphenylene derivatives has attracted considerable attention because the curved pi-surface of these nanographene fragments suggests unusual aromaticity. In this work we report a scalable route that proceeds through a macrocyclic precursor and affords the target in 42% isolated yield. Density functional theory calculations indicate that the strain energy of the hoop is approximately 68 kcal/mol, which appears to facilitate the final reductive aromatization step.

Single-crystal X-ray diffraction reveals a diameter of 12.4 A for the carbon framework, consistent with earlier reports on kekulene analogues. The observed Curie temperature of the molecular ferroelectric sample was 318 K, and the remanent polarization reached 4.2 uC/cm2 under a coercive field of 15 kV/cm. We speculate that the domain switching behaviour is governed by the flexibility of the cationic sublattice, although a contribution from defect migration cannot be excluded.

Results and Discussion

Variable-temperature dielectric measurements were carried out between 120 K and 400 K. The dielectric constant increases sharply near the phase transition, and second-harmonic generation confirms the absence of an inversion centre below the transition temperature. Piezoresponse force microscopy under an applied magnetic field demonstrates reversible domain switching at 200 K.

曲面纳米石墨烯环的可规模化合成

环对亚苯基（CPP）衍生物的合成引起了广泛关注，因为这些纳米石墨烯片段的弯曲 π 表面暗示了其不同寻常的芳香性。本文报道了一条可规模化的路线：经由大环前体进行，以 42% 的分离收率得到目标分子。密度泛函理论计算表明，该环的张力能约为 68 kcal/mol，这似乎促进了最后的还原芳构化步骤。

单晶 X 射线衍射揭示碳骨架直径为 12.4 Å，与先前关于凯库勒烯类似物的报道一致。所测分子铁电体样品的居里温度为 318 K，在 15 kV/cm 的矫顽场下剩余极化强度达到 4.2 μC/cm2。我们推测，畴翻转行为受阳离子亚晶格柔性支配，尽管不能排除缺陷迁移的贡献。

结果与讨论

变温介电测试在 120 K 至 400 K 之间进行。介电常数在相变附近急剧上升，二次谐波产生证实相变温度以下不存在反演中心。施加磁场下的压电力显微镜表明，200 K 时存在可逆的畴翻转。
